ANIRUDH RAVICHANDER

Indian composer and playback singer who has redefined contemporary cinematic soundscapes across Tamil, Telugu, and Hindi cinema. Renowned for chart-topping anthems and electrifying background scores.

Personal Life & Heritage

Born in Chennai to actor Ravi Raghavendra and classical dancer Lakshmi Ravichander. Coming from an illustrious lineage connected to cinema and classical arts, his great-grandfather was pioneering director K. Subramanyam, and he is grand-nephew to Dr. Padma Subrahmanyam.

Education & Sound Mastery

Graduated from Loyola College, Chennai in 2011. Anirudh mastered classical piano at the Trinity College of Music in London and completed sound engineering at Soundtech Media. Early recognition came through an A.R. Rahman judged music competition, paving the path to global stardom.
